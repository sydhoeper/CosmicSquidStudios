The concept

Five chambers for desire, grief, rage, envy, and fear.

Belief Emotion is information

Feelings signal needs, boundaries, attachments, and change.

Practice Architecture supports reflection

Visitors physically enter and sit with an emotion.

Atmosphere Safe, strange, sacred

Organic forms and filtered light make introspection feel protected.

How the chambers connect

Five chambers share a circular foyer. Desire is the developed prototype.

The Desire Chamber

Inspired by the Devil tarot card: pleasure, attachment, and agency.

Core question

Pleasure or compulsion?

Does desire support your life—or control it?

Symbol Loose chains preserve agency

The figures appear bound, but what restrains them can be removed.

Guardian Face the shadow self

A devil figure guards honesty about appetite, sexuality, and control.

Space Make room to sit with desire

Soft alcoves and low light create space for reflection.

The build

Sculpted in VR, textured in Substance Painter, and made walkable in Unity.
