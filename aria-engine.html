ARIA Engine

I populated 200+ rooms in Peter Burr’s digital skyscraper and composed cinematic interstitials.

My roleTechnical artist
Scale200+ rooms populated
ToolsUnity, prefabs, Meshy AI
StatusMulti-year artwork in progress

Slide 1 of 6

A face scan turns each visitor into a character in the “dirtscraper.”

The assignment

Give every room a story within Peter’s visual system.

Direction

One prompt per room

I translated Peter’s AI prompts into scenes.

Constraint

A limited prefab kit

Repeated objects had to feel fresh across hundreds of spaces.

Goal

Make people wonder

Every room needed enough evidence to provoke: “What happened here?”

How I populated the world

  1. Interpret the prompt

    Identify who occupied the room and what happened there.

  2. Stage the evidence

    Recombine prefabs, add Meshy assets as needed, and review with Peter.

  3. Compose the interstitials

    Place two cameras per room to create slow cinematic clips.

What I delivered

Worldbuilding

200+ populated rooms

Each space carries its own premise, aftermath, and visual clues.

Cinematography

Two views per room

A library of slow cinematic clips.

Collaboration

One cohesive vision

Daily review with Peter guided the work.
