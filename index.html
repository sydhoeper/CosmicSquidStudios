About Cosmic Squid

The whole experience, thoughtfully built.

Cosmic Squid Studio is Syd Hoeper’s remote-first studio for immersive learning, training, and exploration.

I bring interaction design, Unity production, and worldbuilding together to shape how an experience feels, flows, and works. My portfolio includes contract collaborations, academic client projects, and independent creative work, with my role credited in each case study.
